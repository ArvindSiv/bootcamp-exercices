In theatre, a shell (also known as an acoustical shell, choral shell or
bandshell) is a curved, hard surface designed to reflect sound towards an
audience.

Often shells are designed to be removable, either rolling away on wheels or
lifting into a flyspace. Shells are most commonly used for orchestras, bands and
choirs, although they can also be used in any application that requires passive
sound amplification. Shells are generally made of hard materials, because they
are designed to absorb as little sound as possible.
